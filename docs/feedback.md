## Run a short review

Show `docs/wireframes.html` on a desktop and a phone, then show the working website. Ask at least two people if available. Obtain their agreement to record anonymous feedback; use labels such as Reviewer A, with no unnecessary personal information. Do not contact people automatically on the student's behalf.

Ask each person to:

1. Explain the site's subject and time period after looking at the Overview.
2. Find an event in the timeline and identify the supporting source.
3. Find what a public cabina was and how access changed with mobile internet.
4. Find information about unequal access and explain what a displayed statistic measures.
5. Move to another page on a phone; try keyboard navigation on desktop if possible.

Ask: “What was unclear?”, “What was hard to read or find?”, and “What one change would help most?” Record observed difficulties as well as their exact words. Avoid leading them toward a preferred answer.
