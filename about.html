A lifetime as
the starting point.

Perú Online explores the internet in Peru from 1997, the author’s birth year, through 2025. The timeline combines contemporary accounts, institutional publications and official statistics. The project was prepared for CM1040 Web Development in 2026.

This is a selective history of public access, fixed and mobile networks, and the geographic divide. It is not an exhaustive history of every provider or policy. The lifetime framing establishes the period; it does not claim personal memories of each event.

Reading the numbers

The access chart uses one INEI measure and one quarter for all four categories. It describes households with internet service, not individual internet users, subscriptions, coverage or speed. Estimates for Q4 2025 are preliminary. The 54.8-point gap is calculated from the published rounded percentages.

DESIGN & CREDITS

Built around
the connection.

The visual direction takes inspiration from the spacious compositions and sculptural 3D motion in Alex Bender / FANCY’s Resadex concept and hero animation concept. Their artwork is not reproduced.

The globe and device illustrations are original procedural graphics created for this project with AI assistance. The globe is decorative, with approximate land shapes and imaginary connection arcs; it is not a network map. Fonts: DM Sans and Instrument Serif, distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Font files are served locally.

The website uses semantic HTML, responsive CSS and browser JavaScript. Historical records are loaded from JSON, validated, then rendered through an escaped-token template engine. Research, code, design and documentation were developed with AI assistance; source links allow readers to inspect the evidence.
